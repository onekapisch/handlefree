# Handlefree compatibility

Last reviewed: 4 October 2026.

## Physically tested so far

- **The developer's Model Y:** all four cabin doors, frunk and trunk work in the app, and Shortcuts were confirmed in TestFlight.
- **A beta tester's Model Y Standard.**

These are individual reports, not an independent certification or a guarantee across Tesla models, years or software versions.

## Features needing broader testing

- Charge-port cover open/close behavior.
- Fresh open/closed position feedback across vehicles and sleep states.
- Watch relaying, locked-phone mode and specific device configurations.
- Action Button routing on supported iPhones and Apple Watch Ultra.
- Additional Tesla models, model years and steering configurations.

The app includes Model 3/S/X/Y appearance choices. Artwork selection does not enable, verify or certify an actuator. Follow the in-app confirmation process for individual controls.

## Requirements and limits

- iOS 17+ (Control Center and Lock Screen controls need iOS 18); watchOS 10+ for the companion.
- An existing Tesla key card for initial vehicle approval.
- A nearby iPhone for both phone and Watch commands; no standalone Watch key in this version.
- Bluetooth reachability is not a precise distance measurement. No fixed distance promise.
- Reader locations vary by vehicle; follow model-appropriate pairing instructions.
- Passenger and rear doors, frunk, trunk, charge-port cover and Apple Watch are part of Handlefree Pro after the 7-day trial; the driver door, lock and unlock are free.
- Handlefree is not available in France.

For troubleshooting, use the [support guide](https://handlefree.app/support/). When reporting compatibility, supply model/year, software version, app version and the particular action. Do not publish a VIN, key or location.
