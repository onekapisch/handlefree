# Handlefree compatibility

Last reviewed: 21 September 2026.

## Confirmed by the owner

On one Model Y, the owner reported that all four cabin doors, frunk and trunk controls work in-app. The owner also confirmed successful TestFlight operation. This is a single owner's report, not an independent certification or a guarantee across Tesla firmware versions.

## Beta features needing broader testing

- Charge-port cover open/close behavior.
- Fresh open/closed position feedback across vehicles and sleep states.
- Watch relaying, locked-phone mode and specific device configurations.
- Action Button routing on supported iPhones and Apple Watch Ultra.
- Additional Tesla models, model years and steering configurations.

The app includes Model 3/S/X/Y appearance choices. Artwork selection does not enable, verify or certify an actuator. Follow the in-app confirmation process for individual controls.

## Requirements and limits

- iOS 17+; watchOS 10+ for the companion.
- Existing paired Tesla key card for initial vehicle approval.
- A nearby iPhone for both phone and Watch commands; no standalone Watch key in this version.
- Bluetooth reachability is not a precise distance measurement. No fixed 10-metre promise.
- Reader locations vary by vehicle; follow model-appropriate pairing instructions.
- France is excluded from the initial beta.

For troubleshooting, use the [support guide](https://handlefree.app/support/). When reporting compatibility, supply model/year, software version, app build and the particular action. Do not publish a VIN, key or location.
